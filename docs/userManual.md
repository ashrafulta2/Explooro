# Explooro User Manual

Welcome to the comprehensive user manual for Explooro. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for every user role within the platform.

---

## 1. Customer Guide

As a customer, you can browse products, interact with sellers, manage your cart, and track your orders.

### 1.1 Browsing and Searching
- **Step 1:** Go to the Explooro homepage.
- **Step 2:** Use the top search bar to find products, categories, or brands.
- **Step 3:** Use the filter panel on the left side of the search results to refine by price, category, or stock availability.

![Product Search](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400?text=Product+Search+Interface)

### 1.2 Managing Your Cart & Checkout
- **Step 1:** Click **"Add to Cart"** on any product page.
- **Step 2:** Click the cart icon in the top right corner to open the Cart Drawer.
- **Step 3:** Review your items, apply any coupons, and click **"Proceed to Checkout"**.
- **Step 4:** Select your shipping address and preferred payment method, then confirm the order.

### 1.3 Digital Warranties
- **Step 1:** Go to your account dashboard and select **"Warranties"**.
- **Step 2:** View all your active and expired digital warranty cards.
- **Step 3:** To file a claim, click on an active warranty and select **"Submit Claim"**.

---

## 2. Saler (Seller) Guide

Salers manage their storefronts, products, and customer interactions.

### 2.1 Setting Up Your Store
- **Step 1:** Log in to your Saler account.
- **Step 2:** Navigate to the **"Store Builder"** from your dashboard.
- **Step 3:** Fill in your store name, bio, and connect your WhatsApp/Facebook accounts.
- **Step 4:** Upload your store logo and banner in the Media Library.

![Store Builder](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400?text=Store+Builder+Dashboard)

### 2.2 Managing Products and Inventory
- **Step 1:** Go to the **"Products"** tab.
- **Step 2:** Click **"Add New Product"** and fill in the title, description, and pricing.
- **Step 3:** Use the **"Margin Projection"** tool to calculate your potential profit based on platform commissions.
- **Step 4:** Save and publish your product to your shelves.

---

## 3. Supplier Guide

Suppliers provide the inventory that Salers sell on the platform.

### 3.1 Fulfilling Orders
- **Step 1:** Log in and navigate to the **"Orders"** dashboard.
- **Step 2:** View incoming sub-orders routed to your warehouse.
- **Step 3:** Print packing slips and mark orders as **"Ready to Ship"**.

### 3.2 Managing Warranty Claims
- **Step 1:** Go to the **"Claims Inbox"**.
- **Step 2:** Review incoming customer claims.
- **Step 3:** Approve claims for repair or replacement, or reject them with a valid reason.

---

## 4. Moderator Guide

Moderators ensure the platform remains safe and compliant with guidelines.

### 4.1 Content Moderation
- **Step 1:** Access the **"Moderation Queue"**.
- **Step 2:** Review reported products, reviews, or live streams.
- **Step 3:** Approve compliant content or remove violating content.

### 4.2 Requesting Elevated Access
- **Step 1:** If you encounter a task requiring Admin approval (e.g., account suspension), attempt the action.
- **Step 2:** A **"Request Access"** modal will appear.
- **Step 3:** Fill in a detailed reason and submit the request to the Admins.

![Request Access](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400?text=Request+Access+Modal)

---

## 5. Editor Guide

Editors manage the visual presentation and content of the platform.

### 5.1 Theme Studio
- **Step 1:** Navigate to the **"Theme Studio"** in the admin panel.
- **Step 2:** Adjust the master color palette and generate consistent UI tokens.
- **Step 3:** Preview the theme and click **"Publish"** to apply it site-wide.

### 5.2 Content Management
- **Step 1:** Go to the **"Content"** section.
- **Step 2:** Update banners, announcements, and localization strings.

---

## 6. Admin Guide

Admins oversee platform operations, finances, and moderator approvals.

### 6.1 User Management and Approvals
- **Step 1:** Open the **"Users"** dashboard to view all platform accounts.
- **Step 2:** Review and approve pending KYC submissions.
- **Step 3:** Go to the **"Approval Inbox"** to grant or deny elevated access requests from Moderators.

### 6.2 Finance Dashboard
- **Step 1:** Access the **"Finance Overview"**.
- **Step 2:** Monitor GMV, platform revenue, and escrow liabilities in real-time.
- **Step 3:** Approve pending payouts to Salers and Suppliers.

---

## 7. Super Admin Guide

Super Admins have unrestricted access to all platform features and configuration.

### 7.1 Role and Permission Management
- **Step 1:** Navigate to **"Access Control"**.
- **Step 2:** Assign roles to staff members.
- **Step 3:** Manage emergency containment protocols and system-wide module toggles.

### 7.2 Audit Logs
- **Step 1:** Open the **"Audit Log"** page.
- **Step 2:** Track all high-risk actions, including permission grants and financial adjustments, across the entire platform.

---

*Note: This manual is a living document and will be updated as new features are rolled out to the Explooro platform.*
